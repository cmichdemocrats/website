# Preface
The Constitution is meant to provide structure to the College Democrats at Central Michigan University. It is not meant to be a document laying out the exact path the organization will take. For leaders, it should be used as a guiding point, but neither a crutch nor a sword. That said, the Constitution should strive to be as minimal as possible. 
 
# Budget
All budget proposals are subject to review by the voting membership. If a simple majority (50% + 1) of the voting membership approves a budget, it will be ratified in the organization. 

# General membership
## Eligibility
Anyone can become a regular member of the organization. 

## Voting members
A regular member becomes a voting member after attending three (3) consecutive general meetings, given that they are a student or the organization’s advisor.

A voting member forfeits their right to vote on organizational decisions after missing three consecutive general meetings. Voting members may, under a simple majority (50% + 1) decide to grant exceptions to members at risk of disenfranchisement in specific instances.

Upon graduating or leaving Central Michigan University, alumni become non-voting members.

The advisor is a permanent voting member while they have their position.

## Roles and responsibilities
*	Ensure that the Executive Board follows the best interests of the organization.
*	Actively participate in meetings and events. 

# Executive Board
## Elected officers
Elected officers include the president, vice president, and treasurer.

## Delegating responsibility to appointed officers
Executive Board members have the authority to delegate their duties to other roles and members. They may appoint people if the appointed roles fall within the scope of their own duties. 

There can be many types of appointed officers, so their responsibilities should not be listed in this Constitution. 

## Term limits
Elected officers are limited to serving at most one term (academic year). Upon vacating office, elected officers cannot serve in any elected position. This disability can be removed by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of the voting membership. 

## Confirmation of appointed officers
Appointed officers will serve in interim until confirmed by a simple majority (50% + 1) vote of the voting membership. If the interim officer is rejected by the voting membership, the interim officer must immediately cease serving.

## Appointed officers and elections
Appointed officers serve until they resign, are impeached by the General Board, or removed by their direct report. They are not immediately affected by elections.

## Override of removed appointed officers
If a two-thirds (2/3) majority of the voting membership decide that an officer’s removal was unjustified, the officer will immediately be reinstated to their position.

## Leadership roles and responsibilities 
### President
The President determines what the organization does. Some basic responsibilities include:
* Determine the mission, vision, strategy, and direction of the organization.
* Chair general board and executive board meetings, unless they appoint someone else to do it.
* Represent the organization in any official context.
* Create an initial draft of the budget.
* Propose suggestions for the budget as needed.
* Fulfill responsibilities of vacant roles.

### Vice President
The Vice President determines how the organization does something. Some basic responsibilities include:
* Serve in the President’s absence.
* Follow the strategy of the President.
* Represent the organization in matters related to operations.
* Oversee internal operations of the organization.
* Maintain an accurate record of the organization’s voting membership.
* Fulfill responsibilities of vacant roles.

### Treasurer
The Treasurer has the authority to determine what money can spent on. Some basic responsibilities include:
* Serve in the Vice President’s absence.
* Maintain an accurate ledger on the organization’s expenses.
* Regularly report transactions to the general board.
* Represent the organization in matters related to finances.
* Create and maintain a budget that encourages fiscal responsibility.
* Review and approve requests based on the budget.
* Grant access to the bank accounts and credit cards if a desired transaction is within budget.
* Secure the bank accounts.

### Advisor
* Advise the organization’s members.
* Oversee all elections.

## Impeachment and removal
Any Executive Board member — elected or appointed — is subject to impeachment and removal. Only voting members may propose impeachment. If a two-thirds (2/3) majority of voting members agree to remove an Executive Board member, the member will be immediately removed from their position.
 
# Elections
## General elections
General elections for the following school year should occur before the end of the first semester. Elections are based on secret ballot voting. Officers will be inaugurated during the last general meeting of the school year.

## Special elections
Special elections are like general elections, except they will happen at the latest one week after an elected office is vacated. Officers will be inaugurated as soon as they are elected. Their term lasts for the remainder of the school year.

## Running for multiple offices
If a candidate runs for multiple offices and wins multiple elections, then they may only pick one role to serve as. If there is a runner-up, then they will win the office that the candidate did not select.

# Amendments
Any regular member can propose amendments to the Constitution; however, only voting members can vote on changes. If a two-thirds majority of voting members agree to a proposed amendment, the amendment must be added to the Constitution and effective within a week of its ratification. 

Amendments must be recorded somewhere and easy for members to access.
 
# Dissolution
If the College Democrats at Central Michigan University dissolves, all funds and assets will be delegated to the Isabella County Democratic Party.
